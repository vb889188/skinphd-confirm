# SkinPhD Confirm PIN process

A PIN is **workspace sign-in only**. It is not a signature and it is not a pack code.

Therapists and witnesses do not collect a PIN. They use a personal link, or they sign on the salon tablet.

Confirm stores a hash, not the PIN. Head Office cannot look up an old number.

## Issue a first PIN (franchisee / Head Office)
1. Staff → New person.
2. Role = Franchisee / manager.
3. Enter a 4–8 digit PIN.
4. Save. Mail opens with email, branch, PIN, and site link.
5. Tell the person to change it under Settings after first sign-in.

## Reset a forgotten PIN
1. Staff → that person → **Email new PIN**.
2. Confirm issues a new 4-digit PIN and opens mail.
3. The old PIN stops working immediately.
4. They sign in and change it under Settings.

## Staff change their own PIN
Settings → current PIN + new PIN.

## How an employee signs and later opens their copy

Therapists do not log into the Head Office workspace. Every employee email says the same three options, in plain language:

1. **At the salon.** Pass the tablet. They type their own legal name. No PIN.
2. **Not at the shop / at home.** Open the personal link on their phone.
3. **After they signed.** The same link is their copy. Keep the email.

Head Office: pack → **Send to their phone — salon, home, or later**, or **Email the employee their copy**.


## Rules
- Do not reuse 2468 after the pilot.
- Do not put PINs in WhatsApp group chats.
- Do not give therapists a workspace PIN unless SkinPhD later asks for staff login.
