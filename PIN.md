# SkinPhD Confirm PIN process

Confirm stores a hash, not the PIN. Head Office cannot look up an old number.

## Issue a first PIN
1. Staff → New person.
2. Enter a 4–8 digit PIN.
3. Save. Mail opens with email, branch, PIN, and site link.
4. Tell the person to change it under Settings after first sign-in.

## Reset a forgotten PIN
1. Staff → that person → **Email new PIN**.
2. Confirm issues a new 4-digit PIN and opens mail.
3. The old PIN stops working immediately.
4. They sign in and change it under Settings.

## Staff change their own PIN
Settings → current PIN + new PIN.

## Rules
- Do not reuse 2468 after the pilot.
- Do not put PINs in WhatsApp group chats.
- A PIN is sign-in only. It is not a signature.
